1. Scope and test basis

Two related workflows in the Checks module: check management (create, edit, pause, resume) and ping handling (status new/up/grace/down/paused/started). The shared record is the Check (timeout, grace, status, last ping, last start, ping count). Twelve functional requirements, four business rules and six use cases are fixed in docs/01-scope-and-test-basis.md. Business rules: BR-01 boundary (60..31536000 s), BR-02 compound status decision, BR-03 state transitions, BR-04 role and key permissions. Roles: owner, rw member, ro member, rw key, ro key, unauthenticated. Full scope and assumptions are in docs/01 and docs/00-brief-notes.md.

3. Risk register and traceability

The 5 × 5 register in docs/05-risk-register.md holds 15 product and 4 project risks. Technique and depth follow the risk level: high risks get MCC/MC-DC, EP/3-point BVA, decision-table and state-transition tests plus mutation and a CI gate. Exit criteria RC-1/RC-2/RC-3 are in docs/06-exit-criteria.md. The traceability matrix docs/07-traceability.csv links FR → BR → RISK → test → defect; tools/traceability/coverage.py computes 12/12 FR = 100% executed coverage from 67 test functions (evidence/raw/requirements-coverage.json, manifest IDs EV-004/EV-005).
